# Assumptions

Current Travel emission factors are static, transparent, order-of-magnitude
estimates. They are intended for relative decision support, not billable carbon
accounting or exact real-world measurement.

## Current Scope

- Transport
- Analogies

## Interpretation

- Emission factors are order-of-magnitude estimates.
- Analogies are scale references, not offset claims.
- Results are not billable carbon accounting.
- Transport values vary by occupancy, vehicle, route, region, energy mix, and
  behavior.

### Limitations

- **Petrol Car**: Does not account for traffic, car size, or exact fuel efficiency.
- **Local Bus**: Actual emissions depend heavily on bus occupancy.
- **Train**: Electric vs diesel trains have vastly different profiles.
- **Short Flight**: Takeoff and landing phases heavily skew emissions for very short flights.
- **Smartphone Charge**: Grid intensity varies heavily by region and time of day.
- **Tree Absorption**: Varies drastically by tree species, age, and climate.
